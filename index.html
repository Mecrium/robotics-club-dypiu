Who We Are

About the Club

We're a community of passionate engineers pushing the boundaries of robotics, automation, and embedded systems at DYPIU.

Our History

Founded under the Semiconductor Engineering Department of DYPIU. Initially led by a single dedicated senior, the club has now flourished under its second committee leadership.

Our Mission

To foster innovation and technical excellence by providing a platform for students to design, build, and test robotic solutions that address real-world problems.

Our Vision

To become the leading robotics club recognized for groundbreaking projects, competitive excellence, and fostering a culture of interdisciplinary innovation.

Our Goals

Quarterly workshops on advanced robotics, national competition participation, and cutting-edge projects in AI, automation, and embedded systems.
